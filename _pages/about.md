I'm Tiger XU — founder, engineer, and ML researcher based in Beijing, China.

As CTO of **CrossNow**, I built a multi-tenant pharmaceutical commercialization SaaS and MaaS platform from the ground up, deployed on AWS EKS. The platform covers HCP profiling, sales force effectiveness (SFE), and LLM agent capabilities for drug commercialization teams.

My research focuses on LLM fine-tuning theory and graph learning robustness. I'm currently working on three papers targeting NeurIPS 2026 and ICLR 2027.

I'm also a Master of Engineering Management (MEM) candidate at the **School of Management and Economics, Beijing Institute of Technology**.

Before CrossNow, I spent nearly 8 years at **Veeva Systems** as a Principal Software Engineer, designing and building CRM SaaS on AWS and multi-tenant architectures. Earlier I worked at **Servier** and **Pfizer** on SFE, CRM, and MDM systems — giving me deep roots in how pharma commercial operations actually work.

## Research Interests

- **Graph Learning** — robustness of Graph Transformers, randomized smoothing
- **Agent Memory Systems** — long-context, retrieval-augmented architectures
- **Pharma & Life-sciences AI** — drug discovery, personalized medicine
